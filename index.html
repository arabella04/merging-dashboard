💡 Why does the 1Life screenshot show "No audit trail entries to display"?

On the 1Life page (Program > Merge Duplicate Account), the Date Range filter defaults to today only (10/20/2026 - 10/20/2026). When no merges have been performed yet today, the table shows 0 entries. Our sync engine automatically widens the Date Range (e.g. 01/01/2026 - 10/20/2026) and scrapes both the Audit Trail and Merged Accounts tabs!
